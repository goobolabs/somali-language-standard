## Gobanimo

*Maxamed Ismaaciil "Barkhad Cas" — 1958*

Awowgey ninkii indhaha tiray,
Ninkii aabbahay addoonsaday,
Ninkii ifka igu adduun tiray,
Itaal li baa ishaa i bidee,
Haddaan ogohoonan ka aarsan karayn
Hangool iyo gudin gar iyo xadhig,
Agalkaa la galiyo dhuxusha giniga ah,
Geedkaa ina deegay gabigeen,
Gadniin Iglan goosan kari maysee,
Isagoo Iglan jooga buu i diloo,
Ushuu soo fidshaa i iimeysoo,
Waa taa aramidii i oofa tirtee,
Asaag li't baa ishaa i bidee,
Ninkaa aragiisa uurkaan ka necbee,
Aan ooyee albaabka ii xidha.

## Aan ooyee albaabka ii xidha

*Maxamed Ismaaciil "Barkhad Cas"*

Wixii arrineen Illaahay jeclayn,
Haddaanan addimada ku oolin karayn,
Aayaadka Qur'aan wada aragtoo,
Udgoone ina yidhi indhaha ka qarshee,
Ninkaa araggiisa uurkaan ka necbee,
Aan ooyee albaabka ii xidha.

Ugaadha ninki dhagee ururshaa,
Ruuxii eegayaa add ka baxshee,
Raggiinnii ogaa horuu u arkee,
Ninkaa aragiisa uurkaan ka necbee,
Aan ooyee albaabka ii xidha.
