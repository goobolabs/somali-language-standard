## Coverage notes

- Ministry/government terminology from the 1972 source and the Mansuur *Ururinta
  1aad* overlap almost entirely; that material is kept once in `06-wasaaradaha.md`.
- *Ururinta 3aad* entries in `09-farsamada-culuunta.md` retain only verified,
  readable English–Somali records; unreadable OCR and Italian-only records are
  omitted. A usable full source (PDF re-conversion or Archivio Somalia extract)
  is required before further curation can proceed.
