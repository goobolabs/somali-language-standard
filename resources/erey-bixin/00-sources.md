The 1987 mathematics glossary ([`05-xisaab.md`](05-xisaab.md)) also includes the
distinct science terms and symbol table from the Mansuur *Diiwaanka* (Ururinta
2aad, 2014) — compiler Cabdalla Cumar Mansuur (Akademiye-Goboleedka
Af-Soomaaliga / Centro Studi Somali, Università Roma Tre). Those terms sit as
`## Ururinta 2aad (Cilmiga)` and `## Sumadaha xisaabta`, separate from the 1987
A–Z list; duplicate English heads across those blocks are kept.

## Coverage notes

- Ministry/government terminology from the 1972 source and the Mansuur *Ururinta
  1aad* overlap almost entirely; that material is kept once in
  [`06-wasaaradaha.md`](06-wasaaradaha.md).
- *Ururinta 3aad* entries in [`09-farsamada-culuunta.md`](09-farsamada-culuunta.md)
  retain only verified, readable English–Somali records; unreadable OCR and
  Italian-only records are omitted. A usable full source (PDF re-conversion or
  Archivio Somalia extract) is required before further curation can proceed.
- Files [`01`](01-bayoolaji.md)–[`09`](09-farsamada-culuunta.md) received the
  2026-08-19 cleanup audit. Approved repairs are in
  [`docs/resource-cleanup/file-reviews/erey-bixin/`](../../docs/resource-cleanup/file-reviews/erey-bixin/).
  That audit is not full scan verification; cleanup review is still open.
