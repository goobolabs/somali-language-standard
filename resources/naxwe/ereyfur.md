# Ereybixinta naxwaha: Soomaali–Ingiriisi

Ereyfurkani wuxuu ururinayaa 268 eray oo ka tirsan **ereybixinta naxwaha
Af-Soomaaliga**. Wuxuu ka tirsan yahay ururinta naxwaha sababtoo ah erayadiisu waxay magac u yihiin fikradaha lagu sharaxo afka, sida *magac* (noun), *fal*
(verb), *weer* (sentence), *yeele* (subject) iyo *layeele* (object).
