# Naxwe — Somali grammar reference

This is an SLS-edited Somali grammar reference built from scanned Somali grammar
books. Its main source basis is the government-era *Aasaaska Naxwaha Af
Soomaaliga* and Abdalla Omar Mansuur and Annarita Puglielli's *Barashada Naxwaha
Af Soomaaliga*. We read the source books, extracted and checked useful
linguistic evidence, then reorganized and rewrote it into a clear SLS structure.

The prose, headings, explanations, examples, links, and tables in these files
are therefore an editorial synthesis prepared by SLS. They should not be read as
page-by-page transcriptions or as wording claimed to be written by the original
authors. The books are the primary evidence; SLS is responsible for the current
selection, organization, condensation, normalization, and explanatory wording.

The collection supplies descriptive evidence for linguistic analysis, AI, NLP,
and machine translation. It is a reference resource, not a learning textbook,
so book exercises and publishing material are excluded.

The collection is part of the SLS [descriptive evidence
library](../../docs/RESOURCES.md), not the normative standard. Normative rules
and compliance decisions belong in the [`spec/` layer](../../spec/0000-index.md).

## Conventions

- Grammar content is Somali-first. Grammatical terms follow the Somali tradition
  (*magac*, *fal*, *weer*, …); an English gloss may be supplied when useful, and
  systematic Somali/English mappings and short Somali explanations are in the
  glossary. The source's Italian column is intentionally omitted from the
  active presentation.
- Grammar content resources are topic-focused SLS references, not facsimile
  transcriptions of source books. Book-only front matter, page furniture, author
  material, exercises, and bibliographies are excluded from chapter content.
- Source material may be reordered, condensed, combined across the two main
  books, and expressed in the established SLS structure. Detailed material
  maintained in a core file is linked rather than copied.
- Source evidence and SLS editorial work are kept distinct: source-supported
  forms are retained where useful, while SLS may clarify wording, normalize a
  documented form, remove OCR damage, or add navigation and explanations.
  Uncertain readings are excluded or explicitly source-labeled rather than
  silently presented as established fact.
- Ungrammatical examples are marked with an asterisk (`*`). The surrounding prose
  identifies whether a judgment is canonical, construction-specific, or an
  attributed source analysis.
- Primary chapter numbering follows a three-part structure:
  - **00–01** — language, thought, word, and sound (lexicology and phonology)
  - **02–08** — noun, determiner, pronoun, numeral, linker/preposition, and verb
    morphology and paradigms
  - **09–12** — simple sentences, noun phrases (OM), complex sentences, and
    sentence types
- [`ereyfur.md`](ereyfur.md) is a Somali–English terminology reference for the
  grammatical concepts used across this collection. It gives a short Somali
  explanation for every term; chapters provide the fuller rules, paradigms and
  examples.
