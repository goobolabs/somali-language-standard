# Sources — naxwe

This registry records the source books and the editorial method behind the
`resources/naxwe/` collection. The core files are not direct transcriptions of
one book. They are an SLS synthesis prepared from scanned source evidence,
with the two books below as the principal grammar sources.

## Principal source basis for files 00–12

| Source role | Source title | Author / institution | Year |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Main grammar source | *Aasaaska Naxwaha Af Soomaaliga* | Guddiga Af Soomaaliga; government-era (Dowladdii Kacaanka) publication | 1973 |
| Main grammar source | *Barashada Naxwaha Af Soomaaliga — A Somali School Grammar* | Abdalla Omar Mansur; Annarita Puglielli | 1999 |
| Terminology source | Glossary at the end of *Barashada Naxwaha Af Soomaaliga* | Associated with the same book; no separate glossary byline recorded | 1999 |

These sources supply the grammar topics, terminology, examples, paradigms, and
descriptive analyses used in the core. The current Markdown wording is not
attributed sentence-by-sentence to either book: SLS selected, compared,
reorganized, condensed, corrected where documented, and rewrote the material
for a consistent reference format.

## Coverage notes

- Chapters [`00`](00-luqadda-iyo-fekerka.md)–[`12`](12-noocyada-weeraha.md)
  and the [grammar terminology reference](ereyfur.md) are topic-focused SLS
  curations based mainly on the two principal source grammars above. They are
  not facsimiles or page-by-page transcriptions. The terminology resource
  preserves the Somali–English pairs and adapts the source's Somali meaning
  column into short explanations; its source glossary also contained Italian.
- The scanned books are evidence sources, not the final SLS presentation. A
  source may establish a topic or form while the current file uses a shorter
  explanation, a combined comparison, a normalized spelling with a recorded
  reason, or a cross-link to another SLS resource.
- The source registry does not claim that every sentence or table cell in every
  core file occurs verbatim in one source. Provenance belongs to the source
  books collectively; editorial wording and organization belong to SLS.
- Supplementary files 13–17 received repository-only whole-file audits and
  conservative SLS-native cleanups on 2026-08-12. The approved records are
  [N13](../../docs/resource-cleanup/file-reviews/naxwe/13-aasaaska-naxwaha.md),
  [N14](../../docs/resource-cleanup/file-reviews/naxwe/14-naxwaha-cusub.md),
  [N15](../../docs/resource-cleanup/file-reviews/naxwe/15-naxwaha-sifayneed.md),
  [N16](../../docs/resource-cleanup/file-reviews/naxwe/16-weeraynta-soomaaliga.md),
  and [N17](../../docs/resource-cleanup/file-reviews/naxwe/17-naxwaha-af-soomaaliga.md).
- Those cleanups removed OCR debris and excluded unrecoverable material rather
  than reconstructing it by inference. They do not constitute page-by-page
  verification against every source scan; current workflow state is recorded
  in the [cleanup tracker](../../RESOURCE_CLEANUP_TRACKER.md).

- *Codaynta Af Soomaaliga* (1977) is curated in the
  [phonology source registry](../phonology/00-sources.md).
