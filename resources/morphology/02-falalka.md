# Falalka — isrogrog iyo baaradigmayaal

Shaxda tixraaca ee **isrogrog** (conjugation classes), **amminka**,
**muuqaalka**, **hababka**, iyo **diidmada**. Faahfaahinta barashada waxay
ku taal [`naxwe/07-sarfaha-falalka.md`](../naxwe/07-sarfaha-falalka.md) iyo
[`naxwe/08-hogatuska-baradigmaha-falalka.md`](../naxwe/08-hogatuska-baradigmaha-falalka.md).

**Ilaha:** S-003, cutub 7–8.
