## Coverage notes

- All seven content files are curated from the 1977 *Codaynta Af Soomaaliga*
  source scan, recovered via OCR and manually verified.
- No audio alignments or modern acoustic measurements — future STT/TTS evidence.
- One English linguistics supplement curated (`08-gariirka-iyo-spread-glottis.md`);
  *On Consonants in Somali Metrics* deferred pending scope decision.
- Full dialect lexicon and comparative dialectology belong in a future
  `dialects/` collection; only phonological variation is covered here.
