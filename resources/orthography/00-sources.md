## Coverage notes

- All five primary content files are curated from *Habka Qoraalka* (1977) and
  manually verified against the source scan.
- **Capitalization** (SLS-0005): curated in `06-xarafka-weyn.md` from Nilsson §2.3
  (interim supplementary source). The 1974 Ministry book was sampled; it
  documents writing/literacy history, not capitalization rules. Revisit if a
  Somali-primary capitalization source is located.
- *Codaynta Af Soomaaliga* (phonology, 1977) is curated in
  [`resources/phonology/`](../phonology/) — it is not orthography evidence.
- Alphabet and phoneme inventory overlap with `naxwe/01-ereyada.md`; that
  material is not repeated here.
